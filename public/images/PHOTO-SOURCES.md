## Licensing evidence and limits

- Unsplash stock photography is generally available under https://unsplash.com/license (free commercial and non-commercial use, with its exclusions); Pexels photography is generally available under https://www.pexels.com/license/.
- The original publisher domains are blocked by the current environment allowlist, so original current license pages and image pages were not fetched directly. These are documented stock image sources; copies and explicit attributions are accessible on GitHub.
- Aletu README credits Melih Karaahmet and links the Unsplash photo ID. Its link URL slug says "black-chevrolet-camaro" although the actual image and README description show two Skyline R34s; therefore preserve the unique photo ID and do not infer the slug's model name.
- j-brzoz README explicitly credits each photographer and Unsplash. Filenames retain unique Unsplash IDs. Avoid claiming its repository software license covers the images.
- Bennyonye filename explicitly retains "pexels-albinberlin-919073". Its README suggests Pexels/Unsplash sourcing but lacks image-specific credits beyond the filename.
- Uncertain-source Ridex/Carvilla assets are also present under their cloned directories but are NOT selected. Their generic "free to use" notices are weaker provenance than the selected stock sources.
